Former President Trump has backed away from a proposal to impose a 20 percent fee on ships transiting the Strait of Hormuz, a critical global energy chokepoint.

Corroborated Facts
Trump rejected a proposed 20 percent toll on ships passing through the Strait of Hormuz.
Trump indicated a shift toward investment deals with Gulf States instead of the fee.
The Strait of Hormuz is a critical chokepoint for global oil trade and shipping.
