Full category (Primary Topic)	Short UI label	Story type	Why it belongs	Examples
Conflict / Flashpoint Topic — Custom	[Custom name]	Named wars, active fronts, recurring regional crises, high-risk bilateral confrontations, maritime flashpoints, or persistent conflict zones that users will expect to track directly. This should override the generic topic when the conflict itself is the organizing frame.	This prevents major ongoing situations from being scattered across generic labels like Military Ops, Diplomacy, Humanitarian Impact, Maritime Security, and Economic Pressure. Users can follow the whole crisis under one intuitive topic.	Russia–Ukraine War · Russia–Ukraine — Ukraine launches major drone attack on Moscow; Russia and Ukraine trade strikes. U.S.–Iran Conflict · U.S.–Iran — U.S. and Iran sign MOU to end hostilities; U.S. lifts Strait of Hormuz blockade after Iran deal; ex-adviser says Iran deal is a setback. Israel–Lebanon Front · Israel–Lebanon — IDF soldier killed by IED in southern Lebanon; Israeli attacks in southern Lebanon kill three. Gaza War · Gaza — over 1,000 fatalities reported since ceasefire; Israel conducts Gaza City drone strike. South China Sea Dispute · South China Sea — Philippines removes platform at Scarborough Shoal. Korean Peninsula Tensions · North Korea — Kim Yo Jong rejects denuclearization; G7 reaffirms North Korea denuclearization. Taiwan Strait Tensions · China–Taiwan — Taiwan urges U.S. arms approval amid Chinese threats. Red Sea Crisis · Red Sea — Houthi attacks, shipping disruptions, coalition maritime response.
Military Operations	Military Ops	Air, land, sea, space, or special operations not better captured by a named active conflict topic. Includes raids, intercepts, military accidents, operational incidents, base attacks, force protection events, tactical developments, and attacks on U.S. forces or allies outside a managed conflict track.	These are direct military or operational developments that may affect force protection, escalation risk, regional stability, commander awareness, or U.S./allied military posture. This category stays useful because not all military operations belong to an active conflict.	Military Ops · Persian Gulf — Apache goes down near Oman and analysts assess air-combat implications. Military Ops · Global — major U.S. special operations raid. Military Ops · Indo-Pacific — unsafe air intercept involving U.S. or allied aircraft. Military Ops · Africa — military base attack or evacuation operation not tied to a managed named conflict.
Force Posture & Deployments	Force Posture	Troop movements, basing, access agreements, deployments, rotations, carrier movements, bomber task forces, exercises, withdrawals, posture reviews, and NATO / regional / Combatant Command footprint changes.	These stories show where military power is positioned and how readiness, deterrence, access, and alliance commitments are changing.	Force Posture · Europe — U.S. reviews military forces in Europe amid NATO criticism. Force Posture · Poland — U.S. considers Polish offer for permanent U.S. military base. Force Posture · Indo-Pacific — U.S. deploys additional naval assets near Taiwan or Philippines.
Defense Policy & Military Readiness	Defense Policy	DoD policy shifts, readiness inspections, recruitment, retention, training, morale, force structure, command changes, service leadership, reserve/National Guard readiness, military pay, and military justice.	These affect the health, structure, availability, and decision environment of the Joint Force, even when no operation is underway.	Defense Policy · South Korea — vice defense chief visits Army’s 2nd Corps to inspect readiness. Defense Policy · U.S. — service misses recruiting targets. Defense Policy · NATO — member states debate readiness requirements.
Weapons, Procurement & Defense Industry	Defense Industry	Arms sales, weapons approvals, defense contracts, production capacity, munitions shortages, shipbuilding, aircraft programs, procurement delays, industrial-base issues, allied weapons transfers, and platform upgrades.	Weapons supply, production capacity, and defense-industrial resilience affect operational readiness, allied support, deterrence, and long-term force modernization.	Weapons & Industry · Taiwan — Taiwan urges U.S. to approve arms sale amid Chinese threats. Weapons & Industry · Global — MQ-9 getting airborne early-warning radar. Weapons & Industry · U.S. — missile production shortfall or shipbuilding delay.
Nuclear, WMD & Strategic Deterrence	Nuclear / WMD	Nuclear weapons, WMD threats, missile tests, arms control, strategic deterrence, nuclear doctrine, chemical/biological threats, IAEA disputes, proliferation, and nuclear facilities.	WMD and deterrence stories carry high escalation risk and are central to senior military and national-security awareness.	Nuclear / WMD · Iran — Iran nuclear program developments. Nuclear / WMD · North Korea — North Korean official calls nuclear arsenal a core interest. Nuclear / WMD · Global — arms-control treaty collapse or WMD proliferation warning.
Cyber, Space & Emerging Technology	Space & Tech	Cyberattacks, cyber espionage, satellite disruption, space-domain activity, AI-enabled warfare, autonomous systems, drones, hypersonics, electronic warfare, quantum, and dual-use technologies.	These stories affect future conflict, command-and-control, infrastructure vulnerability, intelligence collection, military modernization, and strategic competition.	Cyber / Space / Tech · Iran — Pentagon AI chief says Grok chatbot was used to launch missiles at Iran. Cyber / Space / Tech · China — China’s farm drones described as a dual-use security concern. Cyber / Space / Tech · Global — satellite disruption affects military communications.
Foreign Policy & Diplomacy	Diplomacy	State visits, foreign-ministry statements, bilateral meetings, embassy actions, ceasefire talks, diplomatic disputes, aid packages, recognition decisions, negotiations, and crisis diplomacy not already captured by a custom conflict topic.	Diplomacy shapes the strategic environment, alliance behavior, escalation management, military access, sanctions, conflict resolution, and U.S. foreign-policy posture.	Diplomacy · U.S.–Israel — JD Vance tells Israel it cannot “kill your way out” of security problems. Diplomacy · Europe — EU leaders discuss Ukraine and global issues. Diplomacy · U.S.–China — senior officials meet after military-to-military tensions.
Alliances, Partners, & Multilateral Institutions	Alliances	NATO, EU, UN, G7, ASEAN, coalition activity, treaty changes, burden-sharing disputes, joint statements, alliance expansion, partner commitments, and institutional security decisions.	Alliance cohesion and multilateral decisions affect deterrence, basing, burden sharing, coalition operations, sanctions, legitimacy, and crisis response.	Alliances · Europe — NATO allies respond to U.S. force posture review. Alliances · Global — G7 leaders issue joint security statement. Alliances · Indo-Pacific — ASEAN discusses South China Sea code of conduct.
Economic Pressure & Sanctions	Economic Pressure	Sanctions, export controls, embargoes, asset freezes, trade restrictions with security implications, tariffs tied to strategic competition, financial pressure, SWIFT/banking restrictions, and energy-price leverage.	Economic pressure is a major tool of statecraft and can affect adversary behavior, conflict financing, supply chains, energy markets, escalation, and diplomatic leverage.	Economic Pressure · Iran — U.S. sanctions Iranian-linked entities. Economic Pressure · Russia — new export controls target Russian defense industry. Economic Pressure · China — tech export restrictions tied to military modernization.
Intelligence, Espionage & Security Services	Intel / Espionage	Spy cases, intelligence leaks, counterintelligence, covert activity, foreign security-service operations, surveillance, sabotage allegations, classified-material breaches, and dissident targeting.	Intelligence and security-service activity can reveal adversary intent, expose vulnerabilities, affect national-security decision-making, or signal covert competition below the threshold of armed conflict.	Intel / Espionage · UK — two men sentenced for spying for Chinese intelligence against Hong Kong dissidents in the UK. Intel / Espionage · U.S. — classified leak affects military planning. Intel / Espionage · Europe — sabotage investigation tied to foreign security services.
Information Environment & Influence	Information Ops	Propaganda, state-media narratives, disinformation, influence campaigns, adversary framing, proxy amplification, narrative targeting, media manipulation, election influence, and coordinated amplification.	These stories show how governments, proxy networks, or aligned media ecosystems shape perception, legitimacy, public confidence, and support for military or diplomatic action.	Information Ops · U.S. — foreign state media amplifies U.S. domestic unrest narrative. Information Ops · NATO — Russian or Chinese state media frames NATO posture review as alliance fracture. Information Ops · Israel — competing narratives around civilian harm or ceasefire violations.
Terrorism, Insurgency & Non-State Armed Groups	Terrorism / Insurgency	ISIS/al-Qaeda activity, militia attacks, insurgent movements, proxy groups, hostage threats, terrorist plots, cartel-paramilitary activity, and armed non-state actors not already handled by a custom conflict topic.	Non-state armed groups can drive regional instability, threaten U.S. forces or allies, disrupt shipping, create counterterrorism requirements, or act as proxies for state actors.	Terrorism / Insurgency · Niger — gunmen attack Niamey airport. Terrorism / Insurgency · Africa — ISIS-linked group attacks security forces. Terrorism / Insurgency · Middle East — militia attack threatens U.S. facilities outside a managed conflict topic.
Border, Homeland & Domestic Security	Domestic Security	Border operations, homeland-security alerts, National Guard domestic use, domestic terrorism, political violence, extremist plots, civil unrest with security implications, and major law-enforcement operations.	These stories belong when domestic security conditions could affect national mobilization, federal response, defense support to civil authorities, force protection, or adversary narratives about U.S. stability.	Domestic Security · U.S. — “accelerationists” behind foiled drone attack at White House. Domestic Security · U.S. — National Guard deployed during major unrest. Domestic Security · U.S. — border security operation involving federal or military support.
Maritime Security & Chokepoints	Maritime Security	Chokepoint access, naval harassment, piracy, shipping disruptions, port security, undersea cables, maritime militia activity, strait administration, freedom of navigation, and commercial shipping risk.	Maritime disruptions can affect global trade, energy flows, naval operations, deterrence, freedom of navigation, supply chains, and regional escalation risk.	Maritime Security · Persian Gulf — Iran, Oman, and Gulf states define Strait of Hormuz administration. Maritime Security · Red Sea — shipping lanes disrupted by attacks, unless handled as Red Sea Crisis. Maritime Security · South China Sea — naval harassment or maritime militia activity, unless handled as South China Sea Dispute.
Critical Infrastructure, Energy & Supply Chains	Infrastructure / Energy	Power grids, pipelines, refineries, ports, undersea cables, telecom networks, rare earths, fuel supply, defense supply chains, infrastructure sabotage, and strategic energy disruptions.	Infrastructure and supply-chain disruptions can create operational risk, economic pressure, homeland vulnerability, energy insecurity, or constraints on military readiness and logistics.	Infrastructure / Energy · Europe — undersea cable sabotage investigation. Infrastructure / Energy · Global — rare-earth supply disruption affects defense production. Infrastructure / Energy · Persian Gulf — energy infrastructure risk tied to regional tensions.
Humanitarian, Civilian Harm & Displacement	Humanitarian Impact	Civilian casualties, refugee flows, famine, humanitarian corridors, evacuations, aid access, war-crimes allegations, disaster-driven displacement, and civilian infrastructure strikes not better handled by a custom conflict topic.	Humanitarian effects shape legitimacy, escalation risk, coalition pressure, stabilization needs, evacuation planning, information narratives, and international response.	Humanitarian Impact · Africa — mass displacement after regional violence. Humanitarian Impact · Global — UN warns of famine affecting regional stability. Humanitarian Impact · Middle East — civilian evacuation or aid corridor story outside a managed conflict topic.
Governance, Elections & Political Stability	Governance / Stability	Elections, leadership transitions, major legislation, constitutional crises, high court rulings, protests, institutional legitimacy, parliamentary instability, and major political developments with strategic relevance.	These stories belong when political developments affect defense policy, foreign policy, alliance posture, sanctions, national mobilization, domestic stability, or adversary narratives.	Governance / Stability · U.S. — major election-security or transition issue with national-security implications. Governance / Stability · France — government collapse affects NATO or Ukraine policy. Governance / Stability · Taiwan — election outcome could shift cross-strait policy.
Law, Courts & International Norms	Legal & Norms	International law disputes, ICC/ICJ actions, war-crimes investigations, sanctions legality, maritime law, rules of engagement, treaty obligations, and major national-security court rulings.	Legal and norm-setting developments affect legitimacy, military authorities, coalition behavior, sanctions, detention, targeting, sovereignty claims, and the acceptable boundaries of state action.	Law & Norms · Global — ICC or ICJ action related to conflict. Law & Norms · South China Sea — maritime ruling affects sovereignty dispute. Law & Norms · U.S. — court ruling affects national-security authorities.
Public Health, Disaster & Environmental Security	Disaster / Health	Pandemics, biothreats, catastrophic disasters, outbreak response, military installation impacts, mass evacuations, climate-security shocks, and disease outbreaks with operational or regional-stability relevance.	These stories belong when health, environmental, or disaster events affect force readiness, basing, humanitarian response, migration, instability, military support missions, or strategic infrastructure. General health/weather noise should stay out unless it has a security angle.	Disaster / Health · Africa — Ebola cases rise nearly 40% in a week and death toll passes 200. Disaster / Health · U.S. — hurricane damages naval base or military installation. Disaster / Health · Global — pandemic affects deployment readiness or international stability.
Global Markets & Strategic Economics	Strategic Markets	Major energy shocks, global inflation, strategic minerals, currency pressure, food security, shipping costs, adversary economic weakness, macroeconomic instability, and economic movement with security consequences.	These stories belong when economic movement affects conflict financing, sanctions, military readiness, defense production, alliance politics, energy security, supply chains, or global stability. Ordinary market coverage should not qualify unless tied to a security or strategic effect.	Strategic Markets · Global — energy prices surge after chokepoint disruption. Strategic Markets · China — economic weakness affects military spending or regional behavior. Strategic Markets · Europe — defense-industrial supply costs affect NATO readiness.
