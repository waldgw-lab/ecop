Pentagon Confirms 100 Troops Wounded in US-Iran Fighting
Updated Jul 20, 2026, 4:48 PM
• The Pentagon confirmed approximately 100 soldiers were wounded since renewed US-Iran fighting, with most having returned to duty.
• Iran attacked a tanker in the Strait of Hormuz, forcing its crew to abandon ship, following the tenth consecutive night of US strikes.
• The dignified transfer of US troops killed in the conflict has been postponed.
