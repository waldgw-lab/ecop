Proposed Curated Metadata Label Model

Use a simple, flexible label structure:

Primary Topic · Focus Label

The Primary Topic describes what kind of story it is.

The Focus Label describes the country, region, theater, relationship, or global scope the story is about.

This keeps the metadata line short, readable, and more intuitive for users scanning the Curated view.

Focus Label Options

The Focus Label can be one of four types:

Focus type	Use when	Example
Single country	One country is clearly the main focus of the story.	Humanitarian Impact · Gaza
Region / theater	The story affects a broader operating area, chokepoint, or theater.	Maritime Security · Persian Gulf
Two-country pair	The story is mainly about a relationship, conflict, negotiation, strike, dispute, or policy action between two countries.	Diplomacy · U.S.–Iran
Global	The story has no single country or region focus, or the impact is worldwide.	Cyber / Space / Tech · Global
Examples
Military Ops · Russia–Ukraine
Diplomacy · U.S.–Iran
Diplomacy · U.S.–Israel
Maritime Security · Persian Gulf
Humanitarian Impact · Gaza
Force Posture · Europe
Cyber / Space / Tech · Global
Why this model works

This model avoids forcing every story into a single-country label when the story is actually about a relationship, conflict axis, theater, or global issue.

For example:

Ukraine attacks Moscow

is easier to understand as:

Military Ops · Russia–Ukraine

rather than only:

Military Ops · Russia

A diplomatic story between the United States and Iran is clearer as:

Diplomacy · U.S.–Iran

rather than only:

Diplomacy · Iran

A regional maritime story involving the Strait of Hormuz is clearer as:

Maritime Security · Persian Gulf

rather than forcing it into one country.

Plain-language rule

The metadata line should answer two questions:

What kind of story is this?
Where, or between whom, is it focused?

So the Curated card reads as:

Topic · Focus

This gives users a short, scannable label while making the story easier to understand at a glance. The Curated concept already treats UI labels as the visible expression of the story’s categorization, so this is a cleaner display model for the same purpose.
