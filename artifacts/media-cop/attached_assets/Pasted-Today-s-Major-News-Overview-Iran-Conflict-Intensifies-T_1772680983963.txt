Today's Major News Overview
Iran Conflict Intensifies
The U.S. and Israel continue their military campaign against Iran, dubbed Operation Epic Fury, which entered its fifth day. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth announced that the U.S. and Israel are achieving decisive victories and expect to gain complete control of Iranian airspace within days. Iranian ballistic missile launches have dropped 86% since Saturday, with a 23% decline in the past 24 hours alone.

In a historic development, a U.S. submarine sank the Iranian warship IRIS Dena in international waters off Sri Lanka using a Mark 48 torpedo—the first such sinking since World War II. Sri Lankan forces recovered 87 bodies and rescued 32 survivors.

The conflict has resulted in significant casualties, including six U.S. service members killed in a drone strike on a tactical operations center at Port Shuaiba, Kuwait. Four have been identified: Capt. Cody Khork, Sgt. 1st Class Noah Tietjens, Sgt. 1st Class Nicole Amor, and Sgt. Declan Coady.

Regional Impact and Escalation
Iran has retaliated with missile and drone attacks across the region:

A drone struck near the U.S. consulate in Dubai, setting grounds ablaze
Iranian missiles targeted Israel and Gulf states
NATO intercepted an Iranian ballistic missile heading toward Turkish airspace
Israeli strikes hit Hezbollah positions in Lebanon
The conflict has stranded hundreds of thousands of travelers across the Middle East, with major airports closed and widespread flight cancellations. The State Department has evacuated over 9,000 Americans and urged citizens in 14 countries to leave immediately.

Leadership Transition in Iran
Following the death of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei in the initial strikes, Iranian opposition media reports that his son Mojtaba Khamenei has been selected as the new Supreme Leader by the Assembly of Experts, though this hasn't been confirmed by official Iranian sources.

Economic Consequences
Oil and gas prices have surged, with UK gas prices reaching three-year highs and Brent crude oil surpassing $85 per barrel. The conflict has effectively halted tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz, disrupting approximately 20% of global oil supplies. President Trump announced the U.S. International Development Finance Corporation will provide insurance for ships in the Persian Gulf.

Congressional Response
The Senate is set to vote on a war powers resolution aimed at limiting President Trump's military authority in Iran, requiring congressional approval for further action. The measure faces an uphill battle with Republican opposition, though Senator Rand Paul joined Democrats in supporting it.

Additional Developments
U.S.-Ecuador Joint Operations: U.S. and Ecuadorian forces launched joint military operations against narco-terrorist organizations in Ecuador.

China's Political Meetings: China's National People's Congress convened, expected to announce a lower economic growth target of 4.5-5% for 2026 amid ongoing challenges.

Nuclear Energy: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission issued its first commercial nuclear reactor construction permit in eight years to TerraPower for a facility in Wyoming.
