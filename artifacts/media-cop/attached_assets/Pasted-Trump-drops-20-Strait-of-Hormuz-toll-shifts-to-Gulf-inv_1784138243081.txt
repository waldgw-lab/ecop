Trump drops 20% Strait of Hormuz toll, shifts to Gulf investment deals
Updated Jul 15, 2026, 7:50 AM
• President Trump abandoned a proposed 20 percent toll on cargo passing through the Strait of Hormuz in favor of bilateral investment agreements with Gulf countries.
• The U.S. is preparing to resume or intensify a blockade of Iranian ports amid ongoing conflict between the U.S. and Iran.
• Iran has threatened to halt Middle East energy exports in response to U.S. actions, causing oil prices to rise.
