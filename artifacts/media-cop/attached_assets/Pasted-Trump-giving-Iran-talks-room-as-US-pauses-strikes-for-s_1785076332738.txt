Trump giving Iran talks room as US pauses strikes for second day
Updated Jul 25, 2026, 2:38 PM
• The US Ambassador to the UN stated that President Trump is giving Iran talks some room as the United States paused strikes for a second day.
• Iran's army spokesman Mohammad Akraminia warned the US on July 26, 2026, that continued attacks would expand the conflict ahead of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's US visit.
• Airlines suspended flights to Dubai, Riyadh, and Beirut on July 26, 2026, due to the renewed US-Iran conflict.
