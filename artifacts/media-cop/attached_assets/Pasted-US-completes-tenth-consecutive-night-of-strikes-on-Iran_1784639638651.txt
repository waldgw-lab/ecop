US completes tenth consecutive night of strikes on Iran
Updated Jul 20, 2026, 4:48 PM
• The US military conducted its tenth consecutive night of strikes against Iran, targeting military command centers, maritime capabilities, missile and drone launch sites, and air defense systems.
• President Trump warned that Iran would pay for the deaths of American soldiers, following the killing of US troops in Iranian attacks.
• Shipping traffic through the Strait of Hormuz has largely stalled as ships avoid the key oil corridor due to renewed fighting between US and Iranian forces.
