US strikes Iran for fifth consecutive day amid escalating tensions
Updated Jul 16, 2026, 12:42 AM
• The United States conducted airstrikes against Iranian targets for the fifth consecutive day.
• The Pentagon launched strikes aimed at degrading Iran's ability to threaten global shipping through the Strait of Hormuz.
• The United States resumed a blockade on Iranian ports amid the ongoing military operations.
