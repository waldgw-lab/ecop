Xi Calls for Global AI Cooperation as Trump Accuses China of Election Interference
Updated Jul 16, 2026, 7:36 PM
• Chinese President Xi Jinping called for increased global cooperation on artificial intelligence development and governance while warning against any single country dominating the field.
• US President Donald Trump accused China of interfering in the 2020 US election, specifically alleging theft of voter files.
• Trump's allegations contradict US intelligence assessments regarding Chinese interference in the 2020 election.
